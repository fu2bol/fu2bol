South America (1992–1998)

November 13, 2026

The Era#

The years between 1992 and 1998 represented a transformative period for South American football. Economic liberalization, expanding television markets, and increasing player migration strengthened the connection between the continent and Europe’s leading leagues. Yet domestic competitions remained vibrant, producing teams and players that continued to shape the football landscape.

The five squads presented here are not definitive selections. Rather, they are historical portraits intended to capture some of the personalities, tactical ideas, and footballing environments that defined CONMEBOL during this period.

These aren’t meant to be the absolute definitive best of the era. They are simply my personal choices, assembled the way I envisioned them. There is no rigid hierarchy among these players; what mattered most was getting them down on paper, honoring the history, and capturing a feeling. I know plenty of brilliant names couldn’t fit into these five squads, but their essence and spirit are truly present because the pitch of the 1990s was shared by all of them. Enjoy the journey.

Squad V is therefore dedicated entirely to them: a personal tribute to the Brazilian icons of 1992–1998.
