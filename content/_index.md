## Eleven eras. Seventy-six years. One game.

Before football became a global industry, it was already a reflection of the world around it. Through eleven historical eras spanning 1950 to 2026, this archive explores the world that shaped the game and the players who came to define it.

Economies opened and closed. Borders shifted. Television connected distant audiences. Migration changed cities and reshaped identities. Somewhere within these movements emerged the teams, tournaments, and players that defined entire generations.
This website is an attempt to observe those connections.
The squads and selections found here are not rankings. They are historical portraits. Through them, football becomes a window into larger stories about societies, markets, cultures, and people in motion.
Yet this project also begins from somewhere much smaller.

It begins with names written in the margins of notebooks, makeshift squads pencilled onto the backs of school pages, and endless conversations with friends trying to answer a question that never seemed to have a final answer: who belonged in the team, and why? Those moments occupied only a few minutes of a day, but somehow stayed for decades.

Time has carried us into different cities, responsibilities, and lives. Many of those conversations now exist only in memory. This website is my way of returning to them. Not to recreate them exactly, but to keep their curiosity alive.
If those old discussions can find a home here, perhaps they can invite others to pause for a moment and explore the traces left by this remarkable game.

Every era leaves its fingerprints on football.

## fu2bol is dedicated to uncovering them.
